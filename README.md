# Awesome-Patient-Tools
A curated list of apps, websites, and tools that help patients navigate the US healthcare system — organized by the patient journey.

This list collects the ones worth knowing about, sorted by where you are in your care journey.

## Tools and Resources

### Get Covered
- *[HealthCare.gov Local Help](https://www.healthcare.gov/find-local-help/)* — Find Marketplace-certified assisters, agents or brokers who can help you apply for and enroll in health coverage.

- *[Medicaid.gov](https://www.medicaid.gov/)* — Official federal information about Medicaid and CHIP coverage, eligibility, enrollment and state program resources.


### Estimate and Compare Costs
- *[ER Cost](https://www.ercost.com/)* — Compare ER wait-time data and ER visit cost data for supported U.S. metro areas, using hospital-published, patient-reported and price transparency sources where available.

- *[FAIR Health Consumer](https://www.fairhealthconsumer.org/)* — Estimate typical medical, dental, hospital stay and treatment costs using FAIR Health's private insurance claims database.


### Save on Prescriptions
- *[GoodRx](https://www.goodrx.com/)* — Compare prescription prices at nearby pharmacies and get coupons or savings cards.

- *[NeedyMeds](https://needymeds.org/)* — Nonprofit database of programs that help with medication and healthcare costs, including patient assistance programs, coupons, rebates, co-pay help, clinics and a drug discount card.


### Review, Dispute or Reduce Bills
- *[Bill Sherlock](https://www.billsherlock.app/)* — Free hospital and medical bill review for itemized ER or medical bills; helps spot overcharges, compare fair prices and prepare a dispute.

- *[Dollar For](https://dollarfor.org/)* — Nonprofit that helps patients check for hospital charity care and apply for hospital financial assistance for free.


### Financial Assistance and Advocacy
- *[Patient Advocate Foundation](https://www.patientadvocate.org/)* — Nonprofit offering free case management and financial aid programs for eligible patients with chronic, life-threatening or debilitating illnesses and their caregivers.

- *[Undue Medical Debt](https://unduemedicaldebt.org/)* — Nonprofit that uses donations to buy and erase bundled medical debt for financially vulnerable people; they also publish medical debt resources, but do not abolish debt by individual request.


### Learn More
- *[KFF](https://www.kff.org/)* — Nonpartisan nonprofit source for health policy research, polling and journalism on U.S. healthcare costs, coverage, Medicaid, Medicare and insurance.

- *[An Arm and a Leg](https://armandalegshow.com/)* — Podcast and newsletter from health journalists about why healthcare costs so much and what patients can do about bills, insurance and medical debt.


## Know Your Rights

*The laws and protections most patients don't know they have.*

### Federal Protections
- *[CMS Medical Bill Rights](https://www.cms.gov/medical-bill-rights)* — Federal No Surprises Act resource explaining surprise-billing protections, good faith estimate rights and how to submit a complaint.

- *[Medicare Rights](https://www.medicarerights.org/)* — Nonprofit counseling, education and advocacy for people with Medicare, their families, caregivers and professionals who serve them.

## Contributing

Suggestions, corrections, and new tool submissions are welcome. Please open an issue or submit a pull request.

A few ground rules for additions:

- **Patient-first.** The tool should primarily benefit patients, not providers or insurers.
- **US-focused.** International tools are fine if they have strong US coverage.
- **Actually useful.** No vaporware, no tools that exist mainly to harvest leads.
- **Honest descriptions.** One line, plain language, no marketing copy.

See [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md) for details.

## About the Curator

This list is maintained by [FairBilling Advocate](https://fairbilling.org/) — a medical bill advocate who spends every workday helping patients dispute, audit, and reduce their hospital bills. The tools here are the ones that come up over and over in real cases.

If you've worked through this list and still feel stuck — especially on the billing side — that's normal. Some fights need a professional in your corner. Learn more about advocacy services at **[fairbilling.org](https://fairbilling.org/)**.
