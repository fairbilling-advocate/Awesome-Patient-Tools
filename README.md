# Awesome-Patient-Tools
A curated list of apps, websites, and tools that help patients navigate the US healthcare system — organized by the patient journey.

This list collects the ones worth knowing about, sorted by where you are in your care journey.

## List of Tools

### ER Cost
- *[ER Cost](https://www.ercost.com/)* — Check the true cost of ER, time and cost

### Prescription Drugs
- *[GoodRX](https://www.goodrx.com/)* — Compare prescription drug prices at different pharmacies. Get coupons to lower your price.

- *[Needy Meds](https://needymeds.org/)* — Provide detailed list of programs and discounts foor medications.


### Uninsured? You may qualify for Medicaid or an affordable plan 
- *[HealthCare.gov](https://www.healthcare.gov/find-local-help/)* — Get coverage with a very low premium if you don't have insurance.

- *[Medicaid.gov](https://www.medicaid.gov/)* — Check if you qualify for Medicaid.


### Medical Bill Review
- *[Bill Sherlock](https://www.billsherlock.app/)* — Upload your bill with billing codes and get a report. Can be used for ER bills, hospital bills, doctor bill, imaging and lab bills.
  
- *[Fair Health Consumer tool](https://www.fairhealthconsumer.org/)* — Estimate your healthcare expenses, covers medical and hospital costs, body part procedure, dental services and shoppable services.


### Need financial assistance?
- *[Dollar For](https://dollarfor.org/)* — Nonprofit. Use their tool to check if you qualify for financial assistance. They help you apply for financial assistance for free.

## Contributing

Suggestions, corrections, and new tool submissions are welcome. Please open an issue or submit a pull request.

A few ground rules for additions:

- **Patient-first.** The tool should primarily benefit patients, not providers or insurers.
- **US-focused.** International tools are fine if they have strong US coverage.
- **Actually useful.** No vaporware, no tools that exist mainly to harvest leads.
- **Honest descriptions.** One line, plain language, no marketing copy.

See [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md) for details.

## About the Curator

This list is maintained by [FairBilling Advocate](https://fairbilling.org/) — a medical bill advocate who spends every workday helping patients dispute, audit, and reduce their hospital bills. The tools here are the ones that come up over and over in real cases.
